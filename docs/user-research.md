# HORMONA - User Research

## Purpose

The qualitative phase was designed to understand needs, expectations and barriers around hormonal-health monitoring and the HORMONA concept.

## Research themes

- Relationship with the menstrual cycle and hormonal health.
- Current tracking habits and tools.
- Everyday difficulties and symptoms.
- Perception of continuous hormone monitoring.
- Trust, privacy and scientific credibility.
- Desired improvements and useful features.

## Method

The team prepared an interview guide based on open, non-leading questions, conducted qualitative interviews, transcribed responses and extracted recurring themes and divergences.

## Portfolio privacy note

Raw interview transcripts are intentionally not published in this public repository because they may contain personal and health-related information. Only anonymized, high-level insights are presented.
