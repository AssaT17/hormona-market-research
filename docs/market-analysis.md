# HORMONA - Market Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate whether the HORMONA concept addresses a meaningful market opportunity at the intersection of FemTech, connected health and women's wellbeing.

## Market signals retained from the academic study

- Growth of connected health and wearable monitoring solutions.
- Increasing interest in women's health and personalized health tracking.
- Expansion of mobile health applications dedicated to menstrual health, fertility and menopause.
- Stronger expectations around prevention, personalization and continuous monitoring.

## Opportunity areas

- A growing FemTech ecosystem.
- Demand for better understanding of hormonal health.
- Potential collaborations with healthcare professionals, laboratories and wellbeing platforms.
- Differentiation through the combination of a physical sensor and a digital experience.

## Risks and constraints

- Clinical validation requirements.
- Regulation of connected medical devices.
- Sensitive health-data protection and GDPR compliance.
- High R&D and biosensor costs.
- User trust and willingness to adopt a new type of wearable health device.
