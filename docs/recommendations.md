# HORMONA - Strategic Recommendations

## 1. Strengthen scientific credibility
Prioritize validation, sensor precision, transparent communication and a clear scope of use.

## 2. Simplify the experience
Turn complex biological information into understandable, visual and actionable insights.

## 3. Build trust by design
Make consent, privacy, security and health-data governance visible parts of the product experience.

## 4. Clarify positioning
Determine whether the initial offer is positioned primarily as wellbeing/prevention or as a certified medical device.

## 5. Develop progressively
Start with a focused use case, validate adoption, then expand toward healthcare-professional collaboration and additional life-stage products.
